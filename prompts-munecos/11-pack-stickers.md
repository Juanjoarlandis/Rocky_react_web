- **Salida**: `salidas/sticker-<nombre>.png` (uno por sticker) · PNG fondo
  transparente · 1024×1024 · el sticker ocupando casi todo el encuadre
- **Destino en la web**: esparcidos por esquinas de tarjetas, navbar y footer,
  con rotaciones, como pegatinas recién pegadas.

Generar CUATRO imágenes independientes:

## 11a — Script cromado "ROCKY 35"

Die-cut vinyl sticker, thick white die-cut border, of the word "ROCKY 35" in
glossy chrome airbrush graffiti script lettering with dark outline and light
blue reflections, slight red drop shadow, 90s hip-hop chrome bubble style.
Isolated on transparent background, flat sticker, no perspective, no extra
elements. High contrast, clean edges.

## 11b — Badge "ROCKY35 MUAY THAI CLUB"

Die-cut vinyl sticker, thick white die-cut border: vintage athletic club badge
reading "ROCKY35" in big red block letters with white outline and "Muay Thai
Club" underneath in white connected script over the red letters, retro sports
team logo style, flat colors red/white/black. Isolated on transparent
background, no perspective.

## 11c — Globo "35ERS"

Die-cut vinyl sticker, thick white die-cut border: retro globe icon with black
and yellow wireframe continents, and the word "35ERS" in bold black-outlined
yellow varsity letters crossing in front of the globe, 90s rap crew logo style,
flat colors. Isolated on transparent background.

## 11d — Katakana ロッキースリーファイブ

Die-cut vinyl sticker, thick white die-cut border: the Japanese katakana text
"ロッキースリーファイブ" (Rocky Three Five) in two stacked lines of bold black
brush-style characters with a thin red offset shadow, Tokyo streetwear style,
flat colors. Isolated on transparent background, no other elements.
